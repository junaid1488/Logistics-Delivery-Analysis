# Week 1 Scope

## Objective
Define a realistic logistics scenario, identify KPIs, research applicable
data-science methods, and establish an end-to-end analytical roadmap.

## Business problem
Understand logistics delivery performance and prepare a framework for
predicting late-delivery risk early enough to support intervention.

## Methods
- Descriptive analytics
- Classification
- Regression as an optional continuous-outcome approach
- Clustering for exploratory segmentation
- Optimization for resource allocation and intervention

## Leakage principle
Post-outcome delivery information must not be used as predictive input in the
future modeling stage.
